1. What the app is

Rogone Clipper is a personal, non-commercial desktop application that runs locally on its owner's computer. It transcribes a video, suggests short moments, renders vertical clips with subtitles, and lets the user upload those clips to their own TikTok account through TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API.

2. Who can use it

The app is used by its developer for his own TikTok account. It is not offered as a public service, and no account is created with Rogone Clipper itself.

3. Your content and responsibilities

  • You only process and post videos you own or are explicitly authorized to clip and publish.
  • You remain responsible for every post and must follow TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
  • Every post is reviewed and confirmed by the user before it is sent to TikTok. The user chooses the caption, visibility and interaction settings for each post.
