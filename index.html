What is Rogone Clipper?

Rogone Clipper runs locally on my computer. It transcribes a video, suggests short moments, renders vertical clips with subtitles, and lets me publish those clips to my own TikTok account through TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. Every post is reviewed and confirmed before it is sent.
